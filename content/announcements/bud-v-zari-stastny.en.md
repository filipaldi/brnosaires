Poor September gets an unfairly bad rap just because of a few crying kids who hate school.

In reality, the end of summer means your body finally cools down to a normal operating temperature, you can still show off your tanned, toned tango legs outdoors, and you get to reunite with your lost friends.
They suddenly start popping up at milongas, talking about how much they missed Brno tango.
Tango, which is regularly stepping back into your life and theirs.

All those hugs—which you can peel yourself out of way easier than in July—can be found right here:

👉 Tuesday, Sept 1st, 5:30 PM – 9:30 PM: Open-air milonga at Brno Náplavka (round pontoon)
👉 Saturday, Sept 5th, 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM: Skleněnka!
👉 Thursday, Sept 10th, 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM: Tango & pizza at Sesamo Bakery with Rosario – moving to every 2nd Thursday
👉 Thursday, Sept 17th: Adrinela!
👉 Thursday, Sept 24th: Tango & pizza at Sesamo Bakery with Rosario

📖 Don't forget to mark your calendar for the weekend of Sept 19th & 20th: workshops with the super lovely Argentine couple Rodrigo & Majo. Grand milonga on Saturday!
