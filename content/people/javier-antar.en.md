Originally from Buenos Aires, Javier has been dancing tango since 1996 and was trained by world-renowned masters like Gustavo Naveira. His teaching focuses on solid technical foundations, improvisation, and conscious practice to help students discover their natural movement. With over 15 years of experience teaching at international festivals worldwide, he brings a practical and highly effective approach to every class.
