# Milonga at Rockwine

A regular milonga at the **Rockwine** wine bar (Vídeňská 18, Brno). You dance on a circular floor that hides a secret as well as tango does: it sits on old rotunda foundations. Whoever built it back then could hardly have guessed it would one day take to argentine tango.

For good dancing and good music, pour yourself a good wine and pair it with cheese, olives, and whatever else takes your fancy. The kind people behind the bar will be pleased.

## Where and when

- **Place:** Rockwine, Vídeňská 18, Brno
- **Time:** usually from 19:00 to 22:00
- **Organised by:** the Brnos Aires community
