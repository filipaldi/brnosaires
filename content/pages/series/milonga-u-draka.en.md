# Milonga at the Brno Dragon

Milonga u draka is a regular milonga held at the **Old Town Hall in Brno** (Radnická 8). We dance tango under the statue of the legendary Brno dragon, a spot that gives the evening an atmosphere you will not find anywhere else in the city.

The milonga has a clear rhythm to it: traditional tandas, good music, an open community. Come on your own or with friends, for one tanda or for the whole evening. Refreshments are available, and the cloakroom is right by the entrance.

## Where and when

- **Place:** Old Town Hall, Radnická 8, Brno
- **Time:** usually from 19:00 to 22:30
- **Organised by:** the Brnos Aires community
