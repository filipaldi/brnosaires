# Neolonga at Stolárna

Neolonga at Stolárna is a series of irregularly recurring neolongas at **Taneční studio Stolárna** (Olomoucká 14, Brno). Regular in spirit, unpredictable in dates — worth watching the calendar for.

Unlike a classic milonga, you will hear music that tango rarely gets danced to: contemporary tracks, rock ballads, blues, film scores. Same embrace, different soundtrack. Come and find out what moves you.

## What to expect

- Tandas of 3 tracks, classic format
- Music outside the traditional tango orchestras — pick your style
- Open, welcoming atmosphere for beginners and experienced dancers alike
- **Venue:** Taneční studio Stolárna, Olomoucká 14, Brno
- **Entry:** usually 150 CZK
