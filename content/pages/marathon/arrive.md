# Getting to&nbsp;Brno

Brno sits in the sweet spot between Prague and Vienna. Getting here? Easier than a good ocho.

## By Plane

**Direct to Brno:**
Flights from **London** and **Málaga** land at **Brno-Tuřany Airport**. Small, efficient, no drama.

**No direct flight? No problem:**

- **Vienna Airport (VIE)** – 1.5 hours to Brno by DIRECT bus or train
- **Prague Airport (PRG)** – 2.5 hours to Brno by train
- **Bratislava Airport (BTS)** – 1.5 hours to Brno by bus

All three airports connect seamlessly to Brno. You'll barely notice the extra leg.

## By Train or Bus

Brno's [**main train station**](https://www.cd.cz/en/stanice/5433295/) is well-connected to the tango capitals of Central Europe:

- **Prague** – 2.5-3 hours
- **Vienna** – 1.5-2 hours
- **Bratislava** – 1.5 hours
- **Budapest** – 4–5 hours
- **Kraków** – 4–6 hours
- **Berlin** – 7–8 hours

Trains run frequently. Book through [**ČD (Czech Railways)**](https://www.cd.cz/en/) or [**RegioJet**](https://www.regiojet.com/) for comfortable rides with WiFi and coffee.

## By Car

Central Europe's road network makes driving to Brno straightforward. GPS will get you here, parking won't break you.

**From nearby cities:**

- Vienna: 2 hours
- Prague: 2–2.5 hours
- Bratislava: 1.5–2 hours
- Budapest: 3.5 hours
- Kraków: 3.5 hours
- Berlin: 6 hours

Pro tip: Park near the **city centre** and take **tram 9** to Dělnický dům. Saves you the hassle of finding venue parking.
