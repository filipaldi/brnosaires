# Healthy Schedule

**26 hours of dancing.** Your feet might complain, but your heart won't.

In Buenos Aires, they dance all night while reasonable Europeans sleep. We love tango, but we also love waking up without regret. So we built a marathon that won't destroy you.

## No Scheduled Pauses,<br> Civilised Hours

**Why no pauses?** We really, really hate queues. The scheduled pause means everyone rushes to the cloakroom, the bathroom, the registration desk at once. That's not rest. That's stress.

Here, you take your pause whenever you want. Step out for fresh air. Grab food. Sit down. Your break happens on your terms, not when a schedule tells you to queue up with everyone else.

And honestly? Many dancers last year skipped dinner entirely. Rosario's catering was so generous they never got hungry. Your pause might be shorter than you think.

We dance from 1pm till midnight. Early start, early finish. You get proper sleep every night instead of stumbling into breakfast at 6am.

### Everything you need is at the venue.

Seasonal fruits, fresh vegetables, energising snacks, pure water, tea, and coffee are all included. Hungry for something more substantial?  
Grab a proper meal at the on-site restaurant. We're talking €10 for a decent lunch. Affordable, convenient, and you never have to leave.

Stay for the entire marathon. Rest between tandas, refuel when you need to, and keep dancing.
