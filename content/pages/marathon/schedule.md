# Healthy Schedule

**24 hours of dancing.** Your feet might complain, but your heart won't.

In Buenos Aires, they dance all night while reasonable Europeans sleep. We love tango, but we also love waking up without regret. So we built a marathon that won't destroy you.

**Where:** Dělnický dům, Jamborova 3323/65, Brno, Czech Republic. [See the venue](/marathon-venue/).

The milongas from Friday to Sunday afternoon are for marathon pass holders; the Sunday afterparty is open to everyone.

## No Scheduled Pauses,<br> Civilised Hours

**Why no pauses?** We hate queues. The scheduled pause means everyone rushes to the cloakroom, the bathroom, the registration desk at once. That's not rest. That's stress.

Here, you take your pause whenever you want. Step out for fresh air. Grab food. Sit down. Your break happens on your terms, not when a schedule tells you to queue up with everyone else.

And honestly? Many dancers last year skipped dinner entirely. The catering was so generous they never got hungry. Your pause might be shorter than you think.

Afternoons start at 1pm, nights end at 1am. You get proper sleep every night instead of stumbling into breakfast at 6am.

### Everything you need is at the venue

Fruit, vegetables, snacks, water, tea and coffee are included. Hungry for more? The on-site restaurant does a proper lunch for around €10. Rest between tandas, refuel when you need to, keep dancing.
