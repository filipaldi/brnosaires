# Healthy Schedule

**More than 30 hours of dancing.** Your feet might complain, but your heart won't.

In Buenos Aires, they dance all night while reasonable Europeans sleep. We love tango, but we also love waking up without regret. So we built a marathon that won't destroy you.

## Non-Stop Dancing,<br> Civilised Hours

This is a marathon. When one milonga ends, another begins. Continuous dancing from Friday evening through Sunday night. No gaps, no waiting around.

But we're not savages. We start early and finish early, so you get proper sleep every night instead of stumbling into breakfast at 6am.

### Everything you need is at the venue.

Seasonal fruits, fresh vegetables, energising snacks, pure water, tea, and coffee are all included. Hungry for something more substantial?  
Grab a proper meal at the on-site restaurant. We're talking €10 for a decent lunch. Affordable, convenient, and you never have to leave.

Stay for the entire marathon. Rest between tandas, refuel when you need to, and keep dancing.
