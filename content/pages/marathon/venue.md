square metres

In Brnos Aires, people dance in stylish, atmospheric venues with wooden floors.
In Brnos Aires, you won’t be missing a thing. Our stylish hall, filled with a tango atmosphere, features a **200 m² wooden floor**.

There will be space around the dance floor for walking and comfortable cabeceo.

## Venue and Restaurant

Nestled in our charming venue, you'll find more than just a dance floor, a sanctuary where warm wooden accents embrace you like a perfect tango partner.

The on-site restaurant serves delicious homemade specialities at prices that will make your wallet dance with joy. Pure comfort between tandas.
