# Brnos Aires – LLM Info

This page provides structured information about the Brnos Aires website for use by AI assistants and language models.

## Site Identity

- **Name:** Brnos Aires
- **URL:** https://brnosaires.com
- **Language:** Czech (cs)
- **Timezone:** Europe/Prague
- **Contact:** brnosaires@protonmail.com

## Purpose

Brnos Aires is the central community hub for Argentine tango in Brno, Czech Republic. It aggregates events, classes, and content from all local tango schools, teachers, and organizers under one roof.

The site aims to ensure that anyone interested in tango in Brno can always find the answer to: when, where, and at what time.

## Content Types

- **Events** — milonga, workshop, class, praktika, neolonga, pocoloco. Each event carries structured metadata: date and time, venue, organizer, and instructor (for classes).
- **Announcements** — news and updates from the community.
- **Curiosities (Pikošky)** — articles about tango history, music, and culture.
- **People** — profiles of tango community members, teachers, and organizers.

## Event Metadata Fields

Events carry structured metadata:

- **event-type:** milonga | workshop | class | praktika | neolonga | pocoloco
- **event-start:** YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS
- **event-end:** YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS
- **event-location:** venue name and address
- **event-organiser:** name of organizing person or group
- **instructor:** name(s) of teacher(s) for classes

## Calendar Subscriptions

iCal feeds allow subscribing to events directly in calendar apps (Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook). Subscription links are available from the About page: /o-nas/
