## Brnos Aires Tango Weekend is back!

A whole weekend of workshops, live music and milongas that carry on into the night.
Brno's tango community has joined forces to bring you a weekend you will not forget.

Teachers from Vienna, a concert milonga with live music, and workshops for every level.
