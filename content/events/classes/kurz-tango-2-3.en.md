A course for all intermediate dancers. You need to have completed the Tango 1 course, or Tango 2 or 3.
The course is not about the number of figures, but about their connection, playfulness and communication in a pair.
It is also possible to pay for individual lessons.
If you register in advance, you do not need your own partner.
