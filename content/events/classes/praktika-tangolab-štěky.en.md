18 - 19h - open class focused on a specific topic that will be listed on the website or Facebook TangoLab Štěky.
19 - 21h - practice for everyone.
Practice is a space where you have the chance to practice tango well and get it into your body. During the lessons you will gain information and inspiration, learn figures. During the practices you will practice, repeat and consolidate it in a relaxed manner. There are always instructors at the practices, so don't be afraid to ask questions.
