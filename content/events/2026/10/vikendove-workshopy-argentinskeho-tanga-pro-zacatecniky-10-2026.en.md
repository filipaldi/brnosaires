Don't have time to attend regular dance lessons during the week?
Come and learn the content of one regular course during intensive weekend workshops! 🙂
With us, you don't need your own partner or talent!
Don't be afraid of complex figures and steps, we will teach you how to understand each other in a couple and improvise dance, how to perceive the movement of the body and use it in the dance.
Take the first step and turn it into a dance that brings people together in a hug around the world ❤️
WEAR COMFORTABLE SHOES, women, if you want high-heeled shoes and you don't have the original tango, give preference to sneakers or boots, you'll be better off 😊
We definitely recommend a few t-shirts to change into and deodorant 🙂
