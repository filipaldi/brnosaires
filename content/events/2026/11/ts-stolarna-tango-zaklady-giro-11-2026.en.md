A course for all intermediate-level dancers. We focus on a fundamental figure in Argentine tango that you simply cannot do without: the *giro*. There is a vast array of variations—come and get a clear grasp of them while refining your technique.
The course isn't about the number of figures, but rather how to connect them, fostering playfulness and communication within the couple.
Individual lessons can also be purchased.
If you register in advance, you do not need to bring a partner.
