# Ochos and sacadas

On the public holiday we treat ourselves to a full three hours of shared discovery. The ocho as you know it and as you do not, its connection to sacadas, and combinations to take straight onto the dance floor.

The workshop is for intermediate dancers who already know the basics of ochos, up to experienced dancers for whom we have special treats and extra challenges.

It runs from 6 pm to 9 pm, in place of the open class and practica. The special November price is 590 CZK per person.

Sign up by email at tangolabsteky@gmail.com by Sunday 15 November, ideally as a couple. If you sign up without a partner, we will try to pair you with other applicants or put you on the waiting list.
