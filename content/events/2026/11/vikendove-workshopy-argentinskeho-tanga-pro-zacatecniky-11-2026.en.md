# Argentine Tango in One Weekend
(for absolute beginners)

Tango is a conversation between two people in an embrace. **You don't need special talent — even if you've never danced before, in just one weekend you'll learn how to connect with a partner and discover that you're dancing genuine tango.**

## How We Do It
- **No complicated figures or heavy technique.** Tango has no basic step, so we don't teach one.

- **You discover the movements naturally.** Rather than overexplaining with words, we use simple and playful exercises where you figure things out yourself — your body shows you the way.

- **A safe and friendly space.** Nothing is wrong, mistakes help you learn, and no one is going to laugh at you.

- **Personal attention for everyone.** We don't just stand in the middle of the room. We dance with everyone and rotate partners.

## What You'll Experience
**Saturday**

- You'll realize that all you really need for tango is natural walking.

- You dance with someone you didn't even know moments ago, and you understand each other effortlessly.

- You close your eyes, stop worrying about how you look, and just feel the movement.

**Sunday**

- You swap roles and experience what it feels like to lead and what it feels like to follow.

- The movements start clicking in your mind and body, and you begin to improvise.

- At the end, we put on the music and everyone dances through an entire song. No sequences to memorize. Just fun, playfulness, and joy.

## After the Weekend
The weekend is a taste of tango. If it captures your heart (and we really hope it does!), you can continue with our regular classes. Join anytime — right after the weekend or whenever it suits you. The tango feeling is already in your body.
