# Get dancing to tango vals

November topic of the Tuesday open class by TangoLab Štěky.

How does tango vals differ from tango? We will play with its rhythm and try out simple as well as more demanding variations for the dance floor.

The evening runs like every Tuesday at HEX Gallery:

- 6–7 pm open class on the topic (180 CZK)
- 7–9 pm practica, open training for everyone (80 CZK contribution)

You do not need a partner and you do not need to sign up in advance. TangoLab Štěky posts the open class topics on its website and Facebook.
