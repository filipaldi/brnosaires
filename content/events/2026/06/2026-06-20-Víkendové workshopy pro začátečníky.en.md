# Weekend workshops in Argentine tango for beginners

Weeknights never free? Then learn a whole course in a single weekend.

No partner, no talent, no problem. We'll show you how to read each other in the embrace, improvise on the floor, and let the music move you. Take one step, and watch it turn into the dance that's been seducing the world for a century.

Wear comfy shoes. Ladies, unless you've got proper tango heels, trainers will treat your feet far more kindly.
