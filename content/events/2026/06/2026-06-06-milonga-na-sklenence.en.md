# Milonga at Skleněnka - June

The Skleněnka milonga is a regular date every first Saturday of the month, from 6 PM to 10 PM. It didn't quite work out in May, but it's back. Cheer loudly and clear your diary.
"The most beautiful milonga in Central Europe!" someone once declared.
Skleněnka has been part of Brno's tango fabric for over 15 years. So it's a teenage milonga, beloved by all, even as the dancers keep flashing apologetic smiles when the small floor brings them closer than expected. At least we're not far from each other.
Tango at the most magical spot in Brno.
