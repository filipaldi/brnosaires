# Elizabeth Cordone and Matías Rivas from Tango Studio Recoleta

**From Buenos Aires to Brno.**

Elizabeth Cordone and Matías Rivas from Tango Studio Recoleta are coming to Brno.

They bring tango directly from Buenos Aires — a city where tango is still a living language of everyday life. Their style combines the elegance of the salón, deep work with the body, musicality and authentic connection in the couple.

During their stay in Brno they will lead one special open class on Wednesday, and on Thursday and Friday they will be available for private lessons.

**Open Class: June 3rd, 6:00 - 7:00 PM, Sesamo Bakery Brno**
(price 15 EUR per person, paid on site)

Private lessons are a unique opportunity to work intensively and individually on the topics your dance currently needs:

- technique and quality of movement
- musicality and work with rhythm
- embrace and communication in the couple
- elegance and naturalness of movement
- navigation on the milonga
- work with the axis, stability and comfort in the dance

The lessons are led together by a man and a woman, which makes it possible to perceive tango from both roles and experience the movement and the connection directly while dancing with them.

It's not just about the steps. It's about the way to feel tango.
