# Milonga at Skleněnka - September

Milonga at Skleněnka is a regular milonga that takes place every first Saturday of the month from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM. It's back after a summer break. 
The most beautiful milonga in Central Europe! someone declared.
Skleněnka has been part of the Brno tango scene for over 15 years! In other words, it's a teenage milonga that everyone loves, even though they keep smiling apologetically at the dancers
who are closer than they expected on that small dance floor. At least we're not far from each other.
Tango at the most magical place in Brno.
