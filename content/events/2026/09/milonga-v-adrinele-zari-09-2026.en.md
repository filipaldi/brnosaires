The milonga that has been going on for more than 9 years. You dance around a cakes baked by Mrs. Lada. Mrs. Lada stands behind the counter ready to fulfill any sweet wish and sometimes even smiles a little while watching the dancers. 
Rarely do they have so many colorful fish in an aquarium that swim at the pace of the tango and when Pugliese plays, they are not at all distracted. Milonga at Café Adrinela is a certainty of this metropolis.
