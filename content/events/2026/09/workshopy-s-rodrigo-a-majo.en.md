# Tango Argentino workshops with Rodrigo & Majo in Brno

Rodrigo Fonti & Majo Martirena are among the prominent figures of contemporary Argentine tango. Their style combines a deep respect for traditional tango with a modern approach to movement, improvisation and partner communication. Together they create a dance that is technically precise, but at the same time very natural, musical and authentic. In their lessons they teach not only technique, but above all the ability to really dance to music and create an authentic connection in a couple.
