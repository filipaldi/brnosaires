Start the new school year with dancing, meeting and a nice evening by the river.
Dance on the round pontoon on the Brno embankment. Have a coffee or a drink, chat with friends or meet someone you don't know. Enjoy the view of Svratka.
And if you're just watching tango from afar, feel free to come too. You can soak up the atmosphere, get to know the tango community and find out how best to start with tango. Maybe your own tango story will start right here.
Location:📍 Round pontoon | Rullerovo nábřeží – nápřevka na Svratka, Brno
