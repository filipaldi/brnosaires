Don't have time to attend regular dance lessons during the week?
Come and learn the content of an entire regular course during intensive summer workshops! 🙂
With us, you don't need your own partner or talent.
Don't be afraid of complex figures and steps, we will teach you how to understand each other in a pair and improvise dance, how to perceive body movement and use it in dance.
Take the first step and turn it into a dance that unites people in an embrace all over the world ❤️
BRING COMFORTABLE SHOES, women, if you want heels and don't have original tango shoes, give preference to sneakers or boots, you will be better off 😊

8 lessons of 75 minutes each:
