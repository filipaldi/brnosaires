Thursday milonga at Rosario has moved from Wednesday to every second Thursday from 7pm. It will alternate with Adrinela and Rockwine.
The atmosphere will be informal, sometimes there will be a DJ, sometimes there will be a playlist, but you can always count on Rosario, the dance floor, tandas, hugs and an open atmosphere for everyone, no matter what your tango level is.
You can think of it as practice and repeat what you have learned or try new combinations. Or just come and enjoy dancing, meet new people and refresh yourself with delicious Italian cuisine.
Come alone or bring others with you, for example your favorite leaders or followers.
✨ Free entry
🍷 Kitchen open until 8pm
Let Thursdays at Sesame be full of life. 💫
