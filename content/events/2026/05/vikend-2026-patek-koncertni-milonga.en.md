# Gala Milonga with a Concert (Petr Zámečník & Piastra)

A concert milonga with live music by the **Petr Zámečník & Piastra** ensemble, part of the *Moda Tango Project*. The repertoire runs from Piazzolla-flavoured nuevo tango to the distinctive compositions of accordionist and composer Petr Zámečník, where tango need not be musically central and where its only meaning is the one we give it.

The ensemble plays in a chamber line-up: accordion, piano, flute, cello, double bass and percussion:

- **Petr Zámečník** - accordion
- **Ondřej Špaček** - piano
- **Pavlína Kavulová** - flute
- **Anežka Kavalírová** - cello
- **Jaroslav Panuš** - double bass
- **Lukáš Krejčí** - percussion
