Don't be put off by complicated figures and steps. We'll teach you how to understand each other in the couple and improvise as you dance, how to feel the movement of the body and use it.
WEAR COMFORTABLE SHOES. Ladies, if you want a heel and don't have proper tango shoes, go for trainers instead, you'll feel better. 😊
