# Milonga at Adrinela - May

A milonga that has been running for over 9 years.
You dance around the cake display, baked by Mrs Lada.
Mrs Lada stands behind the counter, ready to grant any sweet wish, and now and then she gives the dancers a small knowing smile.
Few places have so many colourful fish in the aquarium, gliding at tango tempo, and even Pugliese doesn't ruffle them.
Milonga at Café Adrinela is one of this city's certainties.
