# Milonga at Rockwine - May
When they built Rockwine on those rotunda foundations, they had no idea it would also take off as a dance floor for Argentine tango. 💃
Quality dance and quality music deserve quality wine 🍷 and a little something on the side. The lovely girls behind the bar will be delighted.
The floor at Rockwine keeps a secret, just like tango. You can uncover it with friends who'll happily hold you close. 🤗 DJ Martin
