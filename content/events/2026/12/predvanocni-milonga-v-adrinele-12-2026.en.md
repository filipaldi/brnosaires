The pre-Christmas atmosphere of the milonga at Adrinela is something no sensible person would ever miss. From now on, we’re taking a slightly more relaxed pace—not every month like we used to, but rather occasionally. Still, dancing right beside the cake display is an experience in itself, and very few manage to resist. Mrs. Lada stands behind the counter, always sensing the exact moment to slice the next cake, happily fulfilling every wish, and occasionally letting a subtle smile slip as she watches the dancers. Few places boast so many colorful fish in an aquarium swimming at a tango pace—and even when Pugliese comes on, it doesn't faze them one bit.
