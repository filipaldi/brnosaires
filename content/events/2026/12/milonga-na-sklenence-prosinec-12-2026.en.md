# Milonga at Skleněnka – December

Skleněnka is back after a break! Join us for one last pre-Christmas session this year at the most enchanting spot in Brno—perhaps even in all of Central Europe.
Skleněnka has been a fixture of the Brno tango scene for 17 years! You could call it an "adolescent" milonga—one that everyone loves, even if they find themselves constantly offering apologetic smiles to fellow dancers who end up a bit closer on the small dance floor than they had anticipated. Well, at least we’re never far apart!
Tango that is warm and intimate.

DJ Terka
