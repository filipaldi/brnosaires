# Open class and practica TangoLab Štěky

Tuesday evenings at HEX Gallery continue.

From 6 to 7 pm, open class focused on a specific topic that will be listed on the website or Facebook of TangoLab Štěky.
From 7 to 9 pm, practica for everyone.
Practica is the space where you can train your tango nicely and settle it into your body. In classes you get information and inspiration, you learn figures. At practicas you calmly train, repeat and cement it. There are always teachers at practicas, so do not be afraid to ask.
