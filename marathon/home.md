## **Nourishment for Dancers**

You raved about it. We're keeping it.

**Seasonal fruits, nutritious vegetables, energising snacks and water** available throughout your dance journey. **Pure water, aromatic teas, and freshly brewed coffee flow freely**, keeping you hydrated, alert, and ready for your next perfect embrace.

## Two hundred square metres

The floor got rave reviews. Exceptional quality wood, the kind that makes every step feel right. We're keeping it exactly as it was.

**200 m² of dance space** wrapped in warm, welcoming light. Walk the perimeter comfortably, enter the ronda from any corner. A relax zone on the stage behind the DJ lets you rest while staying connected to the music and the dance.

There's also a balcony with seating and tables if you want to watch the floor from above. Sometimes the best view is from a distance.

## Cosy

**We aim for about 150 people. Still cosy, still balanced.**

This time, we're watching the door even closer. No snobbery, no attitude, no room for anything that kills the vibe. We're checking dance experience more thoroughly and, just as importantly, how you show up on the floor and off it.

Balanced time for dancing, sleeping and socialising.
Balanced number of leaders and followers.
The right balance of these items will influence your dancing balance.

- **Level:** Experienced, open-minded, easy-going dancers
- **Style:** Mostly closed embrace, softly held, deeply felt
- **Atmosphere:** We curate for character, not just credentials


## The City

Brno sits between Prague and Vienna, small enough to feel intimate, big enough to surprise you. Architecture nerds lose their minds here. Coffee snobs find their people. Wine lovers discover South Moravia, one of Europe's most underrated wine regions, starts right at the city's doorstep. The tango scene? Soft embrace with an edge.

Come for the dancing, stay for the city that doesn't try too hard.

**[Explore Brno](https://www.notion.so/Visit-Brno-28c51b78e73780fca417e7d58b0d3d2b?pvs=21)** – cafés, food, wine, sights worth your time between tandas.

## Getting Here

Brno is easy to reach. Fly into Vienna, Prague, or Brno itself. Trains and buses connect you to the rest of Europe. Once you're here, trams will get you everywhere you need to go.

## Organisers

We're not running a business. We're hosting friends.

This marathon exists because we love the local tango community, and the international crew who keep showing up. We organise because we enjoy working together, not because we need a stage. Expect zero self-presentation, zero long speeches, zero anything that interrupts the music.

We're looking forward to your embrace.
